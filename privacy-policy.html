Blair's Arborist Equipment ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit or interact with our website blairsae.com.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us through our contact form or quote request form. This information may include:

  • Full Name
  • Company Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number (optional)
  • Shipping or Billing Address
  • List of products requested for a quote

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Process and prepare your equipment quote requests
  • Communicate with you regarding your quote or order status

We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your consent, nor do we sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Security and Spam Protection

To keep our site safe and prevent spam form submissions, we use security tools on our forms:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile: We use Cloudflare Turnstile to verify that form submissions are made by human visitors and not automated bots. As a condition of using this invisible security verification tool, we reference and apply the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

4. Analytics

We use privacy-preserving analytics services to understand how visitors interact with our website:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: A privacy-first analytics tool that does not track individual users across sites or collect personal data.
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to track site traffic trends. Google Analytics may set cookies or read browser properties to perform its service.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save products added to your quote request cart. This data remains on your local machine and is not sent to us or any third party until you submit the quote request form.
